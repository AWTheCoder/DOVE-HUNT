DOVE HUNT - Instructions

Objective - Shoot more doves than your opponent before the timer runs out.

Player 1 Controls:
W - Move Up
A - Move Left
S - Move Down
D - Move Right
SPACE - Shoot

Player 2 Controls:
UP ARROW - Move Up
LEFT ARROW - Move Left
DOWN ARROW - Move Down
RIGHT ARROW - Move Right
ENTER - Shoot

Game Features:
- 60-second timer
- Last 10 seconds warning beeps
- Falling doves when hit
- Score tracking
- Leaderboard saving

The player with the highest score when the timer reaches 0 wins.
