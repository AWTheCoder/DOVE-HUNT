DOVE HUNT - Instructions

About the Game
Dove Hunt is a 2-player competitive shooting game where both players control crosshairs and compete to shoot as many doves as possible before the timer runs out. The player who shoots the most doves wins.

Objective - Shoot more doves than your opponent before the 60-second timer runs out.

Player 1 Controls:
W - Move Up
A - Move Left
S - Move Down
D - Move Right
SPACE - Shoot

Player 2 Controls:
UP ARROW - Move Up
LEFT ARROW - Move Left
DOWN ARROW - Move Down
RIGHT ARROW - Move Right
ENTER - Shoot

Game Features:
- 60-second countdown timer
- Timer changes colour as time runs out
- Warning beeps during the last 10 seconds
- Difficulty mode selection (Easy, Medium or Hard)
- Falling doves when hit
- Score tracking
- Automatic leaderboard saving
- Settings menu with different options (e.g. audio mute/unmute option, toggle night mode display)

To Win the Game:
The player with the highest score when the timer reaches 0 wins. The final scores, the winner of that game, and the selected difficulty mode, are saved to the leaderboard
